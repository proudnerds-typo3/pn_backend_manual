# Editor manual example.org

For the editors of example.org: where to find things in TYPO3, which fields
there are, and how to do common tasks step by step.

## Working in TYPO3

### Where to find what

| You want to | Where |
|---|---|
| Manage pages and their content | **Content › Layout** |
| Manage news items and events | **Content › Records** |
| Upload images and documents | **Media** |
| Create or change a form | **Content › Forms** |

### Page title and menu title

Every page has a **Page title**: the heading at the top of the page and the
basis of its web address. If the page needs a different name in the menu, fill
in **Alternative navigation title**.

### New pages

A new page is hidden until you make it visible: tab **Access**, **Page
visible**.

## Home page

The home page has two columns: **Hero** for the banner and **Content** for the
blocks below it.

### The banner

| Field | What to fill in |
|---|---|
| Header | The title on the banner |
| Image | A landscape photo, at least 1920 pixels wide |
| Link | Optional: the page the button leads to |

### The blocks below

Add blocks in the column **Content** with **+ Content**. The order on the page
is the order in the column.

## News items

### Create a news item

1. Open **Content › Layout** and select the page **News**.
2. Right-click the page and choose **New subpage**.
3. Choose **News** as the page type.
4. Fill in the fields and save.
5. Make the page visible.

### Fields

| Field | What to fill in |
|---|---|
| Page title | The headline of the news item |
| Image | Shown at the top and on the card in the overview |
| Teaser | One short paragraph, shown in bold above the text |
| Date | Determines the order in the overview |

## Events

### Create an event

Events work like news items, under the page **Events**, with the page type
**Event**. Fill in the date, the time and the location.

### Registration form

Tick **Registration on this site** to show a registration form on the event.
The registrations are stored in the folder **Event registrations**.

## Menu and footer

### Main menu

The main menu shows the pages directly below the home page that are visible
and not hidden in menus.

### Footer

The links in the footer are records in the folder **Footer**. Change the text
of a link in the record itself.
